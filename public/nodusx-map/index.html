        From requirement to live capacity.

        1. Screening

          Operators submit capacity. Buyers submit requirements. We check each party and each specification before anything is published, and we publish without names or sites.

        2. Technical fit

          Configuration, fabric, storage, power density, location, go-live date, term and payment structure, matched before either side is contacted.

        3. NDA, then names

          Both parties sign before either is identified. Specifications, site and pricing are shared under it.

        4. Direct contract

          Buyer and operator contract with each other. We stay on the deal through acceptance testing and go-live.

        We work with operators and end users directly. Brokers and intermediaries are welcome when they hold a written mandate from the principal, which we will ask to see.

        Not an offer

        Listings are indicative summaries supplied by operators or buyers, or prepared by Nodus from an operator's written offer. They are not offers capable of acceptance, and nothing on NodusX creates a contract. Every transaction is subject to due diligence, a non-disclosure agreement and a written contract signed directly between buyer and operator. Nodus Partners is not a party to that contract, holds no inventory and holds no client funds.

        Accuracy

        Hardware, quantities, prices and go-live dates come from the listing party and can change without notice. Go-live dates are estimates. Prices are indicative, per GPU-hour unless stated, and exclude taxes. Reviewed and Verified describe the checks Nodus made at the time of listing. They are not a guarantee of availability, performance or title, and each party must make its own checks.
